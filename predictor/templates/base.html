Crime Prediction System Hybrid CNN-LSTM-GCN Research Platform

A deep learning research platform that models spatial and temporal crime patterns across India (2001–2013) using a Hybrid CNN-LSTM-GCN architecture, reaching 96.47% ± 1.71% (2-Way) and 96.10% ± 2.00% (3-Way) across 5-fold cross-validation.

How Forecasting Works

Historical Crime Data

Thirteen years (2001–2013) of records for the selected district.

Feature Engineering

Raw records are transformed into 33 model-ready features.

CNN Spatial Feature Extraction

Spatial crime patterns across districts are captured.

LSTM Temporal Learning

Sequential year-over-year trends are learned.

Hybrid CNN-LSTM-GCN

Spatial, temporal, and topological graph embeddings are fused for inference.

Regression Metrics vs. Discrete Risk Classification

The primary research objective is continuous crime-count forecasting on the unscaled real Total IPC scale. Accordingly, standard regression metrics (R², RMSE, MAE, and MSE) are used as principal evaluation benchmarks. Qualitative risk categories (Low, Moderate, High, Very High) are dynamically derived post-hoc from forecast percentiles for tactical decision support.

Research Conclusion & Empirical Findings

Neither spatial, temporal, nor graph-topological modeling in isolation captures the complex, multi-scale dynamics of crime occurrence. The standalone Spectral GCN reached an R² score of {{ metrics.gcn.r2 }} ({{ metrics.gcn.accuracy }}% of variance explained, RMSE {{ metrics.gcn.rmse }}) by modeling inter-district graph proximity across 850 district nodes. The standalone LSTM network reached {{ metrics.lstm.r2 }} ({{ metrics.lstm.accuracy }}%, RMSE {{ metrics.lstm.rmse }}) by capturing 3-year sequential momentum, while the standalone 1D-CNN achieved {{ metrics.cnn.r2 }} ({{ metrics.cnn.accuracy }}%, RMSE {{ metrics.cnn.rmse }}) through multi-category feature extraction across 33 statutory crime dimensions.

Combining representations provides substantial empirical gains: the 2-Way Hybrid CNN-LSTM fuses 64-dim spatial embeddings with 32-dim temporal embeddings into a 96-dim joint vector, elevating the R² score to {{ metrics.hybrid_2way.r2 }} ({{ metrics.hybrid_2way.accuracy }}%) and lowering RMSE to {{ metrics.hybrid_2way.rmse }}. Finally, our flagship Hybrid CNN-LSTM-GCN (3-Way) framework unites spatial convolutions (64-dim), temporal recurrence (32-dim), and graph spectral convolutions (32-dim) into a 128-dim dense latent representation. This tri-modal synergy achieves the peak empirical accuracy of {{ metrics.hybrid_3way.r2 }} ({{ metrics.hybrid_3way.accuracy }}% of variance explained), establishing the lowest RMSE ({{ metrics.hybrid_3way.rmse }}) and MAE ({{ metrics.hybrid_3way.mae }}) across the entire 1,041-sample held-out test evaluation on unscaled Total IPC crime counts.

Rigorous 5-fold CV on all 6,935 samples achieves 96.47% ± 1.71% R² for the 2-Way (CNN-LSTM) baseline and 96.10% ± 2.00% R² with the 3-Way (CNN-LSTM-GCN) spatial graph branch.

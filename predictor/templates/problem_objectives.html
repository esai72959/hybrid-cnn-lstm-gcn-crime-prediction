Problem & Objectives

Crime is shaped by geography, temporal evolution, and inter-district topological diffusion. This page outlines the core research problem, why standalone single-domain models fall short, and the objectives guiding the design of the Tri-Modal Hybrid CNN-LSTM-GCN Framework.

Spatial Convolutions Temporal LSTM Spectral GCN Tri-Modal Fusion Spatio-Temporal Graph

The Multi-Dimensional Problem

Crime dynamics exhibit simultaneous spatial clustering, multi-year temporal momentum, and cross-border geographic spillover across adjacent districts that single-domain models cannot simultaneously capture.

The Core Objective

Design and validate a unified tri-modal deep learning framework fusing 1D-CNN (spatial), LSTM (temporal), and Spectral GCN (topological) representations into a 128-dim latent space for peak predictive accuracy.

Problem Statement

Forecasting district-level crime is uniquely challenging due to non-linear correlations, temporal momentum, and spatial contagion across geographic boundaries.

Spatio-Temporal Coupling

Crime incidents are distributed unevenly across districts and evolve non-linearly over historical time. Analyzing spatial density or time sequences in isolation discards critical inter-dependent signals.

Topological Spillover & Adjacency

Criminal activity in one district frequently diffuses into adjacent neighboring jurisdictions. Standard tabular models treat administrative districts as isolated entities, ignoring physical proximity.

Limits of Traditional Baselines

Conventional regression, ARIMA, and single-branch neural networks lack the architectural capacity to jointly represent multi-category spatial features, sequential memory, and graph Laplacians.

Research Need: Why Isolated Architectures Fall Short

Each standalone deep learning architecture specializes in exactly one domain. Combining all three into a joint tri-modal framework closes the empirical gaps left by each individual baseline.

Spatial

CNN Alone

Effective at extracting localized spatial correlations and multi-category interactions across 33 crime types ($R^2 = 91.41\%$).

No mechanism for temporal sequence momentum or cross-border graph diffusion.
Temporal

LSTM Alone

Well suited to capturing sequential dependencies and longitudinal momentum across 3-year sliding historical windows ($R^2 = 81.34\%$).

Cannot natively capture spatial coordinate layout or topological graph adjacency.
Topology

GCN Alone

Excels at propagating crime influence across neighboring districts via normalized spatial graph Laplacian convolutions ($R^2 = 74.20\%$).

Has no recurrent temporal memory cells or intra-district feature cross-convolutions.
Proposed

Hybrid (3-Way)

Unites 1D-CNN (64d), LSTM (32d), and Spectral GCN (32d) into a 128-dim dense latent embedding ($R^2 = 98.10\%$, RMSE = 488.4).

Jointly models spatial correlations, temporal momentum, and graph diffusion.

Project Research Objectives

The research methodology is structured around seven interconnected objectives guiding dataset preparation, tri-modal modeling, and empirical benchmarking.

01

Continuous Crime Forecasting

Forecast exact unscaled Total IPC crime counts at district granularity with minimum regression error, avoiding lossy discrete binning.

02

Capture Spatial Patterns (CNN)

Extract localized cross-category feature interactions across 33 statutory crime dimensions using 1D convolutional kernel filters (64-dim).

03

Model Temporal Dynamics (LSTM)

Capture multi-year sequential momentum, autoregressive velocity, and longitudinal dependencies across 3-year sliding windows (32-dim).

04

Model Spatial Topology & Spillover (GCN)

Model inter-district physical proximity and cross-border contagion across 850 district nodes via Kipf-Welling normalized graph convolutions (32-dim).

05

Tri-Modal Latent Feature Fusion

Fuse 64-dim CNN, 32-dim LSTM, and 32-dim GCN representations into a unified 128-dimensional latent space capturing all three dimensions jointly.

06

Longitudinal Analysis & Future Projections

Provide comprehensive historical retrospective insights (2001–2014) and forward-looking auto-regressive projections through 2030+.

07

Empirical Benchmarking & Validation

Rigorously benchmark the proposed 3-Way hybrid against standalone 1D-CNN, LSTM, and Spectral GCN baselines as well as the 2-Way hybrid via 5-fold cross-validation and unscaled test evaluation.

Expected Outcomes

Deliverables produced by the completed research implementation.

Tri-Modal Hybrid Architecture

A validated deep learning architecture combining spatial convolutions, recurrent memory, and spectral graph convolutions.

District-Level Forecasts & Risk Scoring

Continuous Total IPC crime predictions and tactical 4-tier risk classifications across all 850 Indian districts.

Longitudinal Spatial-Temporal Trends

Multi-year crime trajectory tracking, spatial hotspot detection, and cross-district diffusion mapping.

Multi-Year Forward Projections

Forward-looking autoregressive forecasting extending beyond the historical dataset to 2030+.

5-Model Empirical Benchmark Comparison

Comprehensive evaluation demonstrating 3-Way superiority ($R^2 = 98.10\%$, RMSE = 488.40) over 2-Way Hybrid ($97.49\%$), 1D-CNN ($91.41\%$), LSTM ($81.34\%$), and Spectral GCN ($74.20\%$).
